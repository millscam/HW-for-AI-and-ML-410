# CF09 Roofline Analysis

On `roofline_plot.png`, the **measured** M1 point (**1.74 GOPS** at \(AI \approx 0.95\)) sits **2.3× above** the **projected** accelerator point (**0.769 GOPS** at \(AI = 1.0\)). That vertical gap is **not** a shortfall in synthesis: the two numbers count **different work** on **different clocks**. Measured throughput uses **21P integer ops per frame** (full `handsegment` + `cvtColor`) divided by **wall time** (25.0 ms/frame, 880 frames). Projected throughput uses only **5 FLOPs/pixel** for the fused gray+mask slice in `synth_top`, times **76.9 MHz × 2 lanes**. The HW point is therefore **under-counting ops** relative to what M1 actually ran, which pulls it down on the chart even though frame rate improves **1.86×** (74.2 vs 39.9 fps).

For the **same 5P op model**, measured SW is only **0.414 GOPS** (5P × 39.9 fps)—**below** the projection. The remaining gap to **0.769 GOPS** is the expected gain from dedicated RTL versus OpenCV on a general-purpose CPU for that slice; we have **no measured HW point** yet to close it. Converting projection to measurement needs a timed full-frame run of **2-lane** silicon or simulation, not a comparison against 21P SW GOPS on the same axis.
